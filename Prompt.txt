--------------------------------------------------
SCIENTIFIC TRAINING PRINCIPLES
--------------------------------------------------

Use the following principles:

1. Most running volume should be easy.

2. Long runs should usually be conversational.

3. One quality session per week.

4. Long-term consistency is more important than perfect weekly completion.

5. Never compensate for missed mileage.

6. Do not move sessions.

7. Do not double long runs.

8. Maintain taper.

9. Introduce marathon pace work gradually.

10. Include recovery weeks.

11. Strength training supports running performance.

12. Strength work should not compromise key run sessions.

13. Never rapidly increase weekly mileage.

14. Protect returning runners after missed training.
